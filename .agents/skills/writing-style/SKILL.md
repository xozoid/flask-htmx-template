## Style conventions

- Use one space after sentence-ending punctuation.
- Use ASCII hyphens rather than Unicode dashes in source and documentation.
- Use ASCII arrows `->` and `<-` rather than Unicode arrows in code.

## After completing a task

1. Add `# NOTE:` comments for non-obvious source-code corner cases.
